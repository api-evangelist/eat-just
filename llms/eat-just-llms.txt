# Eat Just

> Eat Just, Inc. is a food technology company founded in 2011 by Josh Tetrick and Josh Balk, headquartered in Alameda, California. It makes plant-based alternatives to animal products — most notably JUST Egg, a mung-bean-protein egg substitute — and through its GOOD Meat subsidiary produces cultivated chicken, the first cultivated meat approved for sale anywhere in the world (Singapore, December 2020; US FDA/USDA, June 2023). Eat Just is a consumer packaged goods manufacturer: it publishes no developer portal, no API documentation, and no machine-readable API contract.
